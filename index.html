Privacy Policy for Tunzeel Ur Rehman Games

This policy explains how information may be handled in our mobile games. Please read it to understand our practices and your choices.

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 Last Updated: October 13, 2026
01

Introduction

Tunzeel Ur Rehman (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile games published on the Apple App Store.

Our games may have different features, permissions, and third-party services. Therefore, the information collected depends on the specific game you are playing and the features available in that game.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on the game and the services integrated into it, the following information may be processed:

  • Device and advertising information: Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and information used to support advertising services.
  • Usage information: Interactions with advertisements, ad impressions, video views, taps, and other app interactions.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: IP address, crash reports, app performance information, and technical data used to maintain and improve app functionality.
  • Approximate location information: General location may be inferred from an IP address by advertising service providers.
  • Permission-based information: Certain games may request operating-system permissions when specific features require them. The permissions requested can vary between games.

We do not require users to provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or email address merely to play a game, unless a particular game explicitly provides a feature that requires it.

03

Google AdMob and Advertising

Some of our games use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its related software development kit may collect or process information such as IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostic data, and app performance information.

This information may be used for purposes including:

  • Displaying and delivering advertisements.
  • Personalizing advertisements where permitted and applicable.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Preventing fraud and maintaining advertising security.
  • Improving advertising services and diagnosing technical issues.

Google and its advertising partners may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Not every game necessarily uses the same advertising services or configurations.

04

Tracking and Advertising Choices

Where an app tracks users across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising or measurement purposes, we will request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework when required.

You can manage tracking permissions through your iOS device's Settings. Depending on the game and available options, you may also be able to manage advertising consent or privacy choices within the app.

Declining tracking permission does not necessarily mean that all advertisements will disappear. The game may continue to display non-personalized or other permitted advertisements.

05

App Permissions and Device Access

Our games may request device permissions only when particular features require them.

For example, a specific game might request permission for a feature such as notifications, photos, camera, microphone, or location, if that feature is implemented. Other games may not request any of these permissions.

We do not claim to access every type of device information in every game. The actual permissions and data practices depend on the individual app.

You can review or change available permissions through your device's Settings.

06

How Information Is Used

Information processed through our games and their integrated services may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain game functionality.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Improve stability and performance.
  • Detect technical problems, abuse, or fraudulent activity.
  • Respect user privacy choices and applicable legal requirements.

The purposes applicable to a particular third-party service may depend on its integration and configuration within that game.

07

Sharing of Information

We do not share information indiscriminately. However, information may be processed or shared with service providers when necessary to provide app functionality.

These providers may include Google AdMob and, where integrated, other advertising, analytics, diagnostics, or technical service providers.

Such providers process information under their applicable terms, privacy policies, and legal obligations. The third-party services used may differ from one game to another.

08

Data Retention and Deletion

The information retained, and the period for which it is retained, depend on the type of information, the service involved, and applicable legal requirements.

We do not claim to control all information collected directly by third-party service providers. Such providers may retain information under their own retention and deletion policies.

For information or deletion requests relating to data that we directly control, please contact us using the email address provided below. Requests concerning information held by Google or another provider may also need to be submitted to that provider.

09

Children's Privacy

Our games may have different age ratings and intended audiences. We do not knowingly request personal contact information from children through ordinary gameplay features.

Where a game is directed to children or is subject to additional child-privacy requirements, its data collection, advertising, consent settings, and third-party services must be configured in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements.

Parents or guardians who have privacy concerns may contact us using the details below.

10

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. Third-party providers may apply their own security measures to information that they process.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

You may also be able to withdraw consent or change tracking and advertising preferences through your device settings or the relevant service's privacy controls.

To submit a privacy request, please contact us at the email address below.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our games, features, advertising services, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published with a revised “Last Updated” date. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
